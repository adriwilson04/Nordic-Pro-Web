The Coaching Ratio

5 players per coach.
Maximum.

This isn't a number we landed on — it's the foundation of everything. At 5:1, coaches can observe every rep, correct every pattern, and build a genuine picture of each player's strengths and gaps. You are never lost in a group.

Coaching Philosophy

Evidence over
instinct.

Every coach at Nordic Pro operates from the same starting point: observation before assumption. We don't tell you what we think you need. We watch, measure, document — then build a plan from what the data shows.

This isn't a "feel good" environment. It's a demanding, structured training program designed to expose gaps and develop specific solutions. Coaches are mentors — but they are also honest critics.

"A comfortable training environment produces comfortable players. We build the uncomfortable edge — safely, measurably, and honestly."
