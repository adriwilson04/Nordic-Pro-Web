What is the Program

A short, intensive
European experience
with one goal:

Measurable player improvement. You train twice per day in small groups, play competitive matches on weekends, and receive individualized evaluation through Futeval+ and Kinematics Analysis.

This isn't a holiday with football on the side. It's a structured immersion at Campus Vaxholm — the same facility used by Swedish clubs — with coaching built around your individual profile.

Coaching Model

5 players
per coach.
Maximum.

Small groups maximize meaningful repetitions and technical correction — every day. Coaches observe, film, correct, and re-test in short cycles, not once per week. Your targets are tracked in Futeval+ and reviewed in daily check-ins.

Coaching Loop
One cue → one rep → one correction → one retest.
Film it → measure it → adjust it.
Futeval+
Individual Player Assessment

Baseline in weeks 1–2, then regular re-tests throughout the program. Written reports covering strengths, gaps, next actions — shareable for trials and scouting.

Kinematics Analysis
Movement Mechanics

Side-view clips analyzed for joint angles, timing, and speed proxies. Applied to finishing, acceleration posture, change-of-direction, and return-to-play confidence.

Video Analysis
Session & Match Breakdown

Every session and weekend match contributes to your individual video library. Clips feed directly into coach reviews and Futeval+ reports — building your exportable player profile.
