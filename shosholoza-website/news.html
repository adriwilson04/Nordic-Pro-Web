News & Updates

Latest developments from the Shosholoza pathway

March 2026

Skärgård IK Formation

Official establishment of our Stockholm-based competitive club platform, providing South African players with direct access to Swedish football.

We are proud to announce the official formation of Skärgård IK in the Stockholm Archipelago. This marks a significant milestone in the Shosholoza pathway, providing concrete evidence that our four-stage development system delivers real professional opportunities. South African players will now train and compete alongside Swedish talent in a structured European football environment.

January 2026

Pelikan Heights Facility Secured

Long-term lease secured for our Cape Town training grounds at Pelikan Heights Sports Grounds.

Shosholoza FC has secured a long-term lease for the Pelikan Heights Sports Grounds at 52 Gannet Street, Pelican Heights, Cape Town. This facility will serve as the permanent home for our Skilz School and Shosholoza FC teams, providing dedicated training pitches and match venues. The lease provides stability for our Cape Town operations and allows us to invest in facility improvements with confidence.

December 2025

Pathway Expansion Planning

Strategic planning underway for regional expansion of the Shosholoza model across Southern Africa.

The Shosholoza leadership team has begun strategic planning for regional expansion. Our Cape Town program is deliberately designed to be replicable, and we are exploring opportunities to establish Skilz Schools and club structures in other Southern African markets. This expansion will maintain our quality standards and mission alignment while extending opportunities to talented players across the region.
