Stage 1

Skilz School

Ages 8-12 | Pelikan Heights, Cape Town

Our foundational skills development program introduces young players to European and UEFA-informed fundamentals in small-group settings of 10 players. The internal Skilz School League provides structured match experience through color-coded teams competing in 7v7 Sunday matches.

  • ✓Ages 8-12 development focus
  • ✓Small-group training (10 players max)
  • ✓UEFA-informed methodology
  • ✓Internal league competition
  • ✓Talent identification for Shosholoza FC

Stage 2

Shosholoza FC

U12-23 | Cape Town Tygerberg Football Association

Competitive club football registered with the Cape Town Tygerberg Football Association and SAFA. Our teams train at Pelikan Heights Sports Grounds and compete in structured league and cup competitions while being tracked through our Futeval+ performance evaluation system.

  • ✓Competitive SAFA-registered teams
  • ✓Futeval+ performance tracking
  • ✓Professional coaching staff
  • ✓Player dashboards and scouting reports
  • ✓Diversity and inclusion mission

Stage 3

Skärgård IK

Stockholm Archipelago, Sweden

A competitive club platform in the Stockholm Archipelago where South African players progressing through our pathway train and compete alongside Swedish talent in a professional European environment.

  • ✓Stockholm-based competitive platform
  • ✓Integration with Swedish football system
  • ✓Professional training environment
  • ✓Cultural and language immersion
  • ✓Pathway to Nordic Pro representation

Stage 4

Nordic Pro

Professional Scouting & Placement

Professional player development, scouting, and placement services operating on a non-profit model. Fees are reinvested into future development, supporting the entire pathway ecosystem.

  • ✓Player readiness assessment
  • ✓Professional scouting network
  • ✓Placement from Ettan through Allsvenskan
  • ✓Non-profit representation model
  • ✓Data-driven development tracking
